A BLAST-style DNA identification tool on Solana. A lab submits an unknown DNA sequence, and GeneLedger matches it against a shared reference library on-chain, then translates it into its protein. Rival

Out-move your friends. Winner takes the pool.

Rival turns fitness into a competition. Friends put in a USDC entry fee, earn points from Oura-synced workouts, and a program on Solana pays out the prize pool automatically, so no friend has to hold the money and nobody can run off with it.

  • Competition rules and the payout split are locked on-chain before anyone joins, and the program checks a signed result before paying out.
  • Sign-in is just an email: embedded wallets and automatic fee funding mean players never see a seed phrase or a gas prompt.
  • QuickNode Streams push the program's events to our server in real time, and Gemini Prediction Markets power bonus pick rounds.
  • A "Proof of Sweat" engine scores workouts on duration, effort and evidence, with results anyone in the group can verify.
